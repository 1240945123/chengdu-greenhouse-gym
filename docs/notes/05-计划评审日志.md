## Round 1 - Codex

The plan is scientifically ambitious but not implementation-ready; several
current repository behaviors directly contradict it.

1. **Weather resampling is broken:** `load_weather_data()` overwrites requested
   `h` with source cadence, so hourly ERA5 data will exhaust during a 15-minute,
   180-day episode (`glassgym/environments/utils.py:93`).
   **Fix:** Preserve requested timestep separately, resample to exactly
   `ceil((season+forecast)/h)` rows, and add cross-year/leap-year length tests.
2. **Weather caching can return incompatible arrays:** the cache key omits
   season length, prediction horizon, timestep, and disturbance count
   (`glassgym/components/weather.py:31`).
   **Fix:** Include every load argument plus source checksum in the cache key,
   or cache only immutable raw frames.
3. **The proposed split conflicts with hard-coded protocol assumptions:**
   current code requires 120-day training seasons and labels 2025 spring as
   validation, not test (`benchmark_protocol.py:70`,
   `run_chengdu_benchmark.py:188`).
   **Fix:** Enumerate and test every config, provenance, fingerprint,
   completeness, training, and reporting migration required for the new roles.
4. **Test leakage remains possible:** Sobol screening across multiple years,
   emulator design, weather-generator validation, and threshold justification
   are not explicitly prohibited from using 2025 information.
   **Fix:** Add a lineage matrix restricting fitting/screening/tuning to 2023,
   selection to 2024, and making every 2025 diagnostic non-decisional after
   protocol freeze.
5. **Six approximately 180-day cycles may overlap physically:** two crops per
   year leave almost no clearing interval, and autumn may overlap spring.
   **Fix:** Validate non-overlapping occupancy with transplant and clearing
   buffers, or identify cycles as independent counterfactual simulations.
6. **The primary model contains uncalibrated wet-pad cooling coupled to roof
   ventilation:** `uVent` also applies an 18 kW fan-pad term
   (`glassgym/models/ChengduPhysics/ode.py:73`).
   **Fix:** Remove pad cooling from the primary four-action model and add a
   separately calibrated pad action and energy balance only in the secondary
   experiment.
7. **The safety layer cannot enforce advertised rules:** rainfall is absent
   from disturbances, and pad fan/pump actions are absent from the action
   schema.
   **Fix:** Specify precipitation, independent fan/pump states, interlocks,
   defaults, and backward-compatible schema versions.
8. **Realistic forecast is undefined while MPC reads exact future
   disturbances:** `mpc.py:131` and `observations.py:147` expose future truth.
   **Fix:** Define a timestamped forecast-provider interface, issue-time
   semantics, an archived or predeclared error model, and anti-leak tests.
9. **Solver failures are unsafe and unobservable:** the environment catches all
   exceptions, retains stale state, and still computes reward
   (`greenlight_env.py:286`).
   **Fix:** Catch explicit exceptions, emit structured metadata, terminate
   before normal reward, and test deterministic fallback/timeout semantics.
10. **Crop/harvest accounting is undefined end-to-end:** continuous GreenLight
    harvest and cohort scheduled removal risk double removal and double reward.
    **Fix:** Declare one fruit ledger and prove per-step and season dry-matter
    conservation across allocation, loss, standing fruit, and harvest.
11. **The Bayesian workflow lacks an executable statistical model:** outputs,
    longitudinal likelihoods, measurement error, missingness, source weighting,
    emulator discrepancy, and random-effect identifiability are unspecified.
    **Fix:** Predefine equations and units, with synthetic recovery, simulation-
    based calibration, and leave-one-dataset-out tests before real fitting.
12. **The proposed inference overstates independent sample size:** two 2025
    seasons cannot support general controller-ranking inference, and seeds or
    descendant variants are not independent replicates.
    **Fix:** Treat 2025 as paired descriptive cases or obtain more held-out years
    before population claims.
13. **Acceptance gates can be chosen post hoc:** thresholds and citations are
    deferred, potentially until after 2025 has been examined.
    **Fix:** Version and freeze metrics, thresholds, estimands, exclusions, and
    multiplicity families before evaluation.
14. **There is no concrete implementation and verification map:** outcomes are
    named without files, schemas, migration order, tests, compatibility, or
    stage-stop criteria.
    **Fix:** Define file-level tasks, failing tests first, explicit inputs and
    outputs, rollback-compatible versions, resource estimates, and go/no-go
    gates.

## Round 2 - Codex

Most prior findings are now addressed: substrate repair, cache isolation, split
migration, occupancy, wet-pad separation, disturbance/action schemas, forecast
leakage, solver failures, fruit ledger, descriptive statistics, and file-level
packages are materially improved.

1. **Data-role rules conflict:** Step 5 permits fitting only on 2023, but
   `ForecastProviderV2` is fitted on 2023-2024, while pre-2023 weather has no
   permitted lineage role.
   **Fix:** Define separate `historical_fit_reference`, `fit`, `select`, and
   `retrospective` roles and permitted operations.
2. **The climate gate is effectively vacuous:** Stage A is already simulation-
   only, so that label does not stop controller rankings from a failed model.
   **Fix:** Make gate failure block paper-profile comparisons and publication
   claims, allowing only diagnostic plumbing runs.
3. **Climate validation lacks a data-sufficiency gate:** approximately 2,650
   local hourly records beginning April 2026 cannot establish autumn/winter or
   broad actuator-response validity.
   **Fix:** Require seasonal coverage, missingness limits, extreme-condition
   counts, and actuator excitation, or narrow/defer validation.
4. **The observation likelihood can double-count harvest evidence:** fitting
   batch harvest and its deterministic cumulative sum duplicates measurements.
   **Fix:** Fit primitive increments only and use cumulative harvest for checks,
   or declare full joint covariance.
5. **Emulator and transfer discrepancies remain non-identifiable:** two free
   variance terms can absorb the same residual structure.
   **Fix:** Estimate emulator covariance on held-out simulator runs and freeze
   or tightly inform it before source-transfer discrepancy.
6. **The reward still double-rewards production:** growth and terminal harvest
   value the same biomass and can alter ranking with horizon or discount.
   **Fix:** Use an exact potential-based/telescoping equation or terminal harvest
   alone and test invariance.
7. **Safety projection semantics remain ambiguous:** restriction application
   order can change executed actions.
   **Fix:** Freeze deterministic, idempotent projection order and conflict rules
   with property tests.
8. **RL uncertainty reporting is underspecified:** the interval over five seeds
   has no estimand, and `performance ceiling` overstates a selected seed.
   **Fix:** Define conditional seed-variability summaries and rename it
   `validation-selected seed`.
9. **Reproducibility stops at a dependency manifest:** solver/native versions,
   threading, hardware, and determinism can change results.
   **Fix:** Add an exact lock/container specification and complete runtime
   metadata to `ProtocolFreezeV2`.

## Round 3 - Codex

All nine prior findings are substantially addressed. New or remaining problems:

1. **Weather resampling lacks variable semantics:** generic interpolation of
   radiation or precipitation breaks conserved quantities and extremes.
   **Fix:** Use conservative accumulated-flux allocation, integral-preserving
   interval means, bounded state interpolation, and conservation tests.
2. **Chronological roles are incorrectly universal:** external crop datasets
   and post-2025 local climate records do not fit 2023/2024 roles.
   **Fix:** Make roles pipeline-specific for weather/controller, local climate,
   external crop, and future target harvest.
3. **Log-scale harvest likelihood excludes genuine zero batches:** dropping zero
   and below-detection records biases timing.
   **Fix:** Distinguish structural zero, censored/detection-limit zero, positive
   observation, and missing data in a zero-inclusive model.
4. **Discrete AR(1) is wrong for irregular dates:** row-order correlation ignores
   elapsed time.
   **Fix:** Use continuous-time AR(1)/Ornstein-Uhlenbeck correlation or restrict
   discrete AR(1) to gap-segmented regular grids.
5. **Posterior uncertainty does not reach controller comparison:** ranking one
   fitted model can favor a controller.
   **Fix:** Pair frozen posterior parameter draws and discrepancy scenarios

6. **Initial crop state is unspecified:** identical is fair but may not be
   seasonally or biologically plausible.
   **Fix:** Define `InitialCropStateV2` with state, uncertainty, provenance, and
   paired sampling rules.
7. **Sobol screening is underdefined:** output vector, scaling, distributions,

## Round 4 - Codex

The eight prior findings are addressed. The posterior-robustness layer exposes
six material cross-stage gaps:

1. **No climate posterior is defined:** Step 15 requires climate draws while
   Step 7 has deterministic gates only.
   **Fix:** Define climate uncertainty inference and diagnostics, or accurately
   call the result a bootstrap ensemble.
2. **MPC has oracle plant access:** current MPC uses `env.F` and true `ctx.p`,
   which other controllers cannot observe.
   **Fix:** Separate hidden `PlantModelV2` from frozen `ControllerModelV2` and add
   latent-parameter leakage tests.
3. **MPC rollouts may omit universal safety:** scoring unexecutable raw actions
   gives MPC a false internal optimum.
   **Fix:** Use identical safety projection and actuator dynamics in prediction
   and execution, based only on issue-time information.
4. **Fixed solver-failure penalty is exploitable:** early truncation can beat a
   long negative valid return.
   **Fix:** Make failure strictly dominated under frozen return bounds and add
   adversarial failure-seeking tests.
5. **Posterior robustness lacks a precision gate:** 30 draws and material
   reversal are undefined.
   **Fix:** Predefine direction, Monte Carlo precision, batch stability, and a
   maximum compute budget.
6. **Independent Sobol priors may generate impossible combinations:** correlated
   physiological constraints invalidate classical independent-input indices.
   **Fix:** Transform independent latent factors into a valid joint prior or use
   dependence-aware sensitivity analysis.

   report parameter and process uncertainty separately.
